# Competitor Card: Loóna + Mesmerize

Compiled 2026-09-01. Two sibling apps in sleep/relaxation: Loóna (sleepscapes: interactive color-by-tap scenes + stories + music) and Mesmerize (visual meditation: hypnotic visuals + narration + psychoacoustic audio). Both are subscription sleep/wind-down products, not focus-music tools — but Mesmerize's interactive-visual layer and Loóna's multisensory engagement loop are directly relevant to the venture's visual layer idea.

## Identity

**Loóna: Sleep, reduce anxiety**
- What it is: bedtime "sleepscapes" — interactive tap-to-color scenes paired with a story and ambient music, designed to transition the brain into sleep. Won an Apple Design Award (2021). [verified] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lo%C3%B3na
- Platforms: iOS (App Store id 1465238901) and Android. iPhone-first. [verified]
- Company: Loona Inc (Dover, DE / Palo Alto roots; founded 2019 by ex-MSQRD founders, incl. Eugene Nevgen). Part of the Palta portfolio (Flo, Prisma, Simple). [verified] https://belprauda.org/en/profile/yuri-gurski/
- Pricing: free download, minimal free tier (1–2 sleepscapes), 3–7 day trial, then subscription. Historical: $12.99/mo, $39.99–$59.99/yr. 2026 reviews cite $20/mo and $99.99/yr charges — prices were raised while content stalled. [verified App Store IAP list / user reviews]

**Mesmerize - Visual Meditation**
- What it is: full-screen hypnotic generative-looking visuals + narrated meditations/sleep stories + "psychoacoustic" music. Open-eye meditation niche.
- Platforms: iOS (id 1461517107), Android, tvOS, visionOS, macOS. [verified App Store listing]
- Company: Rockwell Ventures Inc (Fort Lauderdale, FL). Launched May 2019. No public funding footprint — reads as a bootstrapped/holding-company app business.
- Pricing: free download, 7-day trial with aggressive early paywall (~13 seconds into onboarding per UX teardown), then $4.99/wk, $12.99/mo, $49.99–$59.99/yr. [verified App Store IAP list; https://screensdesign.com/showcase/mesmerize-visual-meditation]

## What users praise (recent US App Store reviews)

- The multisensory, interactive wind-down genuinely works — including for ADHD brains. "I have ADHD and the sleepscapes offer a wonderfully multisensory (audio/visual/tactile) experience that helps to focus and relax the mind." (4*, Loóna, Aug 2026)
- Unique content moat, still unmatched: "they have music that i couldnt find anywhere else, & they have bedtime stories! (my absolute favorite)" (5*, Loóna, Aug 2026)
- Mesmerize visuals/narration quality: "the variety of the Narrations and visuals are simply better… For the past 5 years, this is the app I fall asleep to." (5*, Mesmerize, Jul 2026)
- Real emotional dependence: "I'm actually crying as I write this review… this app truly helps calm me down when I need it most." (5*, Mesmerize, Aug 2026)

## What users complain about

- **Abandonment is the #1 Loóna complaint.** "Great app, developers completely gave up" (3*, May 2026); "Exactly when did the developers abandon this app?" (1*, Jul 2026); "Hasn't been updated in over a year!" (1*, Aug 2026). Loyal users beg for new sleepscapes: "it's been so long since they've updated with a new one and it's such a shame" (4*, Aug 2026).
- **Trial/billing dark patterns dominate Mesmerize's 1-star reviews.** "Said it would give me a 7 day trial, but charged me immediately" (1*, Feb 2026); "I was charged for a full year despite signing up for a trial and despite cancelling immediately" (1*, Aug 2026). Roughly 2/3 of Mesmerize's 50 most recent reviews are 1-star billing/paywall complaints.
- **Hard paywall, tiny free tier on both.** "Everything is now Locked behind a Paywall… it costs nearly $20 a month" (1*, Loóna, Jul 2026); "Can't even get into the app without starting a subscription" (1*, Mesmerize, Aug 2026). Reddit echoes this: r/loonaapp "WARNING! This app scammed me out of $65" thread on confusing trial toggles; r/Meditation users hunt free alternatives citing €64/yr Mesmerize pricing.
- Player/engineering decay (Mesmerize): audio player stops when an AirPod disconnects; forced-fullscreen browsing regressed UX in 2026 update (2–3* reviews, Jun–Jul 2026).

## Recent moves 2024–2026

- Loóna is in maintenance mode by design: founders pivoted to XR projects and laid off much of the team in 2023; product stays live "on pause" with slow content additions. [verified] https://belprauda.org/en/profile/yuri-gurski/
- Despite the freeze, Loóna kept tuning monetization: paywall/pricing config updated Sep 2024 (Adapty breakdown), and 2026 reviews show price points up to $99.99/yr — squeezing the installed base, not growing it. [verified] 
- Last Loóna app update: 2025-03-21. No new sleepscape content in over a year per user reviews.
- Mesmerize is the actively-maintained sibling: updates through Jul 2026, expanded to visionOS/tvOS, marketing site refreshed Jul 2026. [verified App Store lookup]
- No evidence of any acquisition/merger between Rockwell Ventures (Mesmerize) and Loóna/Palta — they are unrelated companies that happen to be adjacent. [verified — no press, no database link]

## Threat read

- **Copy the multisensory ritual, not the catalog.** Loóna proved interactive audio-visual engagement (tap-to-color + story + music) creates deeper habit and loyalty than passive listening — users still return nightly years in. A calm interactive visual layer on top of generative focus music is validated; both apps show users treat it as identity-level ("the app I fall asleep to for 5 years").
- **Avoid the monetization reputation trap.** Both brands' review sections are dominated by trial-scam and paywall complaints, and Reddit actively routes users to free alternatives. Aggressive gating bought short-term revenue and permanently torched brand trust — a new entrant with an honest free tier and transparent trial wins these refugees cheaply.
- **The opening: an abandoned, loyal audience.** Loóna's users explicitly ask for novelty ("my brain needs novelty") and new content — exactly what a generative engine produces infinitely at zero marginal cost. Loóna can't serve them (team gone, static handcrafted content); a generative focus app with a Loóna-grade visual layer directly absorbs that orphaned demand.
