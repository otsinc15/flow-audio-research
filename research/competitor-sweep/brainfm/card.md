# Competitor Card: Brain.fm

Compiled 2026-09-01. Sources: Apple iTunes lookup/RSS APIs, DataForSEO Google Ads search volume, Perplexity sonar-pro via OpenRouter (raw JSON saved alongside this card).

## Identity

- **What it is:** "Functional music" app — audio tracks engineered for focus, relaxation, sleep, and meditation. Pitches itself as science-backed (NSF-funded research, patented "neural phase locking" / amplitude-modulation approach). Strong ADHD positioning since 2025 ("ADHD mode").
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, web player.
- **Pricing:** Free download with paywall. Official pricing page: **$14.99/month or $99.99/year**, no lifetime plan [verified — brain.fm/pricing]. Older pricing (2018): $6.95/mo, $48/yr, $150 lifetime — price has roughly doubled [verified — CNET, 2018]. Users frequently cite $70–$100/yr and complain.
- **Company:** BrainFM, Inc. Founded ~2014 [estimate — company profiles]. Launched iOS app October 2016. Team of roughly 13–15; LinkedIn band 11–50 [estimate — LinkedIn/Growjo].

## What users praise (recent App Store reviews)

- **It works for focus, especially for ADHD/neurodivergent users.** This is the dominant 5★ theme — people say it "occupies the wandering part of my brain" and gets them into deep work fast.
  - 5★: "I have ADHD. Worth every penny… I noticed when I play one of the motivation pieces, I get really sharp and focused right away. And then I can stay there in the zone for a long, long time."
  - 5★: "If you have a neurodivergent brain, this app is a lifesaver! …it occupies the 'wandering' or distracted part of my brain with non-lyric, interesting, non-repetitive looping jams so I can actually get things done."
- **Sleep mode is a strong second use case.** Multiple reviews use it nightly; some switched from other apps and report better sleep.
- **Good UX and real music.** 5★: "The UI is absolutely gorgeous… And the focus music/audio? I'm left completely speechless." Another: "This goes beyond mere binaural beats to actually provide real music developed by both scientists and artists."

## What users complain about (recent App Store reviews)

- **Price and paywall greed.** The single biggest complaint cluster. $70–100/yr with aggressive paywalls.
  - 2★: "I enjoyed the app… but they are completely out of their skull thinking that they can charge $100 for this!"
  - 4★: "I absolutely love this app… they just charge you for every single thing like no need to be so greedy."
- **Billing/subscription dark patterns.** Multiple 1★ reviews: no trial without subscribing, hard to cancel, surprise yearly renewal, unresponsive support, spam email that ignores unsubscribes.
  - 1★: "I bought a month for a promotion for a dollar, they never told me my renewal was coming and I was charged for the yearly subscription… they never replied back."
- **Playback stability bugs.** Tracks stopping mid-session, playback errors on removed tracks — described as "negating the purpose of the app" when in the zone.
- **Repetition / thin catalog control.** No way to exclude track categories ("textural soundscape" = "nails on a chalkboard" for one user); Reddit users note tracks get repetitive with daily use.
- **Doesn't work for everyone.** Some find it indistinguishable from free lofi ("It's just music… sounds just like every other lo-fi"), and some ADHD users with anxiety find the stimulation *raises* anxiety.
- **Removed workout mode** — a long-time subscriber is angry they killed a beloved mode.

## Threat read

- **Copy the positioning, not the hype ceiling.** Brain.fm proves a small team (~15 people, <$1M raised, ~$2.5M/yr est.) can build a durable subscription business on functional focus audio. The ADHD/neurodivergent angle is its growth engine — "occupy the wandering brain" is language that converts. Its adjustable "neural effect" intensity dial and in-app session timer are worth borrowing.
- **Avoid the monetization reputation.** Its 1★ cluster is nearly all billing: paywall-everything, surprise annual renewals, hard cancellation, ignored support. And at $99.99/yr, ~1,300 Americans google "brain.fm review" every month looking for a reason not to pay. A generous free tier, honest trial, and sub-$99 pricing (or a lifetime option, which users explicitly miss from 2018) is a direct wedge against it. Also: never ship playback instability — "stopped mid-session" is the complaint that cancels subscriptions.
- **The opening: variety and the visual layer.** Two recurring knocks on Brain.fm are repetition (daily users notice loops, can't exclude categories) and an audio-only experience with "underdeveloped" UX. Truly generative, high-variety techno directly attacks the repetition complaint, and a calm interactive visual layer is something Brain.fm simply doesn't have. Reddit consensus is that good lofi/YouTube gets you "80–90% of the effect for free" — so the moat must be *felt* difference: demonstrably adaptive, never-repeating audio plus a visual ritual, priced under $99, beats "science-backed but samey and expensive."
