# Competitor Card: Calm & Headspace (adjacent-giants benchmark)

Scope note: only their focus-music/audio features matter for the venture. Both are meditation-first giants whose music/audio is one content pillar, not the product.

## Identity

### Calm
- Meditation, sleep, and relaxation app. Music and soundscapes are one of three content pillars (meditations, Sleep Stories, music/soundscapes).
- Platforms: iOS, Android, web. Also Calm Health app (healthcare channel) and Calm Sleep app.
- Pricing: free with subscription IAP. US: ~$14.99–16.99/mo, ~$69.99–79.99/yr, lifetime $399.99–499.99 (prices vary by channel; [estimate], Calm plans page + 2026 pricing trackers). Calm Business / Calm Health are custom enterprise quotes.
- Company: Calm.com, Inc. Founded 2012, San Francisco. App launched Feb 2013.

### Headspace
- Meditation and sleep app plus mental-health platform (Headspace Health, after 2021 merger with Ginger). Focus music is a dedicated "Focus" tab.
- Platforms: iOS, Android, web. Also Headspace XR on Meta Quest (2024).
- Pricing: free download, almost everything paywalled. US: $12.99/mo (7-day trial), $69.99/yr (14-day trial), family $99.99/yr, student $9.99/yr, lifetime ~$399.99 [verified — App Store listings/reviews]. Coaching add-on ~$99.99/mo; insurance-covered therapy. B2B/EAP priced PEPM (~$7–8 per employee per month cited in one EAP comparison [estimate]).
- Company: Headspace Inc. / Headspace Health. Founded 2010, Santa Monica. App launched Feb 2012. Merged with Ginger 2021.

## Focus-music / audio feature detail (the part that matters)

- Calm Music (own help center, support.calm.com article 115005144193): dedicated Music section under Discover. Categories: Focus and Flow, Uplift, Piano, Ambient and Atmospheric, Electronic, Classical and Strings, Soundscapes. Static curated tracks, plus branded exclusives (Moby ambient album, Alanis Morissette). Soundscapes are loopable nature recordings. Not generative.
- Headspace Focus tab: ~12 focus music "stations" (e.g. Lo-Fi Times, Corner Booth Jazz) curated by Chief Music Officer John Legend; ambient, lo-fi, binaural beats, cinematic focus music from Hans Zimmer. Sleepcasts are 45–55 min narrated audio. Not generative.
- Neither offers endless/adaptive generative audio, session-state adaptation, or an interactive visual layer. Music is a retention feature inside a meditation library.

## Threat read

- **Copy the retention mechanics, not the product shape.** Both giants prove that endless, reliable audio (all-night looping soundscapes, 45-min sleepcasts, daily-ritual voices) is what people pay $70/yr for. When Calm's v7 broke soundscape looping, users cancelled within days — playback reliability and uninterrupted looping are existential features, not polish. Ship rock-solid gapless loop + sleep timer on day one.
- **Avoid their two open wounds: billing trust and bloat.** The loudest complaints on both apps are paywall rage, free-trial bait-and-switch, no-refund policies, and forced AI/feature sprawl. A clean, fairly-priced, single-purpose focus app is a positioning weapon against both — and don't force an AI companion into the center of the UI.
- **The opening: neither can do generative depth.** Their focus music is static, celebrity-curated playlist filler inside meditation libraries whose downloads have fallen 30–74% from peak. Neither has generative, adaptive, endless music or any interactive visual layer — and their brand identity (guided meditation) blocks them from leading with it. A venture that does one thing — high-variety generative focus audio plus a calm visual — owns a category the giants treat as a tab.
