# Competitor Card: Noisli

Date: 2026-09-01. Sources: Apple iTunes APIs (US + DE storefronts, live), DataForSEO Google Ads search volume (live), Perplexity sonar-pro synthesis (links below).

## Identity

- What it is: a background-noise generator and ambient sound mixer. You layer rain, wind, fire, coffee-shop chatter, and white/pink noise into a custom mix for focus, sleep, and relaxation. No AI, no neuroscience claims, no generative music — static looped samples with manual sliders.
- Platforms: web app (the core product), Chrome extension, iOS app, Android app. Offline playback on mobile.
- Pricing:
  - Web free tier: 16 sounds, capped at 1.5 hours/day of streaming, 3 playlists. [verified] https://www.noisli.com/pricing
  - Web Pro: $10/month billed yearly (1 user). [verified] https://www.noisli.com/pricing
  - Web Business: $24/month billed yearly for 2 users, scales per user. [verified] https://www.noisli.com/pricing
  - iOS app: one-time $1.99 purchase. [verified] App Store lookup.
- Company: Noisli Labs UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Leipzig/Berlin, Germany. Founders: Stefano Merlo (managing director) and Sabine Staggl (design). Founded 2013, iOS app launched May 2014. [verified] https://about.noisli.com/impressum/ , App Store lookup.

## What users praise

From the 50 most recent US App Store reviews (live RSS fetch):

- Simplicity and the mix-your-own-sounds model. "I love Noisli. I've used it for years. The ability to make my own ambient sound track is amazing." (5★)
- Ad-free, no-nonsense, one-time price. "The fact that it is ad free and so simple to use is such a breathe of fresh air." (5★)
- Effective noise masking for sleep and focus. "By far the best background noise app, especially when coupled with noise canceling headphones and earplugs." (5★)
- Remarkable long-term loyalty. Multiple reviewers have used it for 10+ years (originally via a 2014 Starbucks promo) and still use it nightly. "Over a decade later I still use it every single night before I go to sleep. My most used and most loved app I've ever had." (5★)

## What users complain about

- Abandonment. The iOS app hasn't been updated since 2017 and it shows: "App hasn't been updated in three years and now it's crashing on launch 100% of the time on my iPhone XS... I don't expect it to be fixed." (2★) "Is this app still supported? The last update was years ago." (3★)
- Crashes and dead-on-launch after iOS updates. "App crashes on launch every time. What a bummer." (1★)
- Modern-device fit: not adapted to iPhone X-and-later screens, no AirPlay/HomePod/casting. "Won't output sound to another device such as an Apple HomePod... Wasted $1.99." (1★)
- Thin sound library vs. rivals (28 sounds max). "For an app I paid for, I expected a lot more sound options." (2★)
- Reddit/forums: the 1.5 hr/day free-tier paywall introduction drew real backlash ("Noisli now costs money, any alternatives?" — r/productivity), and users routinely migrate to myNoise for variety or Endel/Brain.fm for adaptive/neuroscience audio. https://www.reddit.com/r/productivity/comments/g1is8f/

## Threat read

- Not a threat — a fossil. Noisli is a two-person team maintaining a 2017 codebase, earning ~$80K/yr. It will never out-build a generative-audio venture. What it proves is that the simple "focus sound" category sustains a paying niche for a decade with near-zero investment: the demand is durable and underserved, not the company.
- What to copy: the things users still rave about after 10 years — one-tap simplicity, mix-your-own layers, no ads, no forced account, honest pricing. Also note the web-first + Chrome-extension distribution: a meaningful share of focus-listening happens at desks, not phones, and the web free tier is the acquisition funnel.
- Where the opening is: every top complaint is a greenfield. Noisli users beg for more sounds (variety), updates (a maintained app), casting/AirPlay, and modern device support — and Reddit's answer to "Noisli alternative" is already Endel/Brain.fm/myNoise. A generative "Endel-type techno" app with high variety directly answers the #1 complaint (tiny static library) of a proven-paying audience, while Noisli's 4.16 rating at $1.99 shows even a stale product holds the simple-mixer segment. Copy their simplicity and pricing honesty; win on variety, freshness, and active development.
