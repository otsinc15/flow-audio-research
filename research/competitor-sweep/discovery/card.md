# Competitor Discovery Sweep — Functional / Generative Focus Music & Sound

Date: 2026-09-01. Target: ALL-OTHERS-DISCOVERY.
Known cards already exist for: Endel, Brain.fm, Focus@Will, Mubert, myNoise, Noisli, Portal, Loóna, Mesmerize, Aimi, Calm, Headspace. All products below are NEW discoveries.

Method note: initial sonar-pro sweep returned uncited output; every entry below was verified against the Apple iTunes Search/Lookup API (hard rating data) or web search. Sonar-suggested products that could NOT be verified are listed at the bottom as rejected. App Store numbers are US storefront unless noted. Brand search volume is monthly, Google Ads data via DataForSEO (2026-09-01).

## Tier 1 — Direct functional-music competitors with real traction

### 1. Moongate: Binaural Beats
- **What it is:** Goal-based soundscapes (sleep / focus / calm / meditate) combining binaural beats with curated music, nature sounds, and noise. Independent team (Moongate Studios, Inc., moongateapp.com). Launched Nov 2023.
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android.
- **Pricing:** Freemium; subscription paywall (screensdesign.com teardown: "114-screen onboarding, $200k/mo").
- **Traction:** App Store 4.66 rating, 59,197 ratings (US). Adapty estimate (Oct 2024): ~90k downloads/mo, ~$200k revenue/mo [estimate]. Site claims "over 50,000 5-star reviews." Brand search volume US: 0 — growth is paid-UA driven, not organic brand.
- **User sentiment:** Praised for simple goal-first UX ("pick a goal, get a soundscape"); complaints typical of aggressive paywall apps.
- **Source:** adapty.io/paywall-library/aura, moongateapp.com, screensdesign.com/apps/moongate-binaural-beats.

### 2. TeraMuse
- **What it is:** Adaptive focus music that responds to your **typing rhythm** in real time — ambient layers while thinking, fuller composition in flow. Uses real music (claims 10,000+ tracks), not AI-generated; web-based DAW and track import. Closest live product to "interactive adaptive focus music."
- **Platforms:** macOS, Windows (free desktop download); web; iOS announced ("free for the launch period").
- **Pricing:** Free tier; paid plans unlock full library + Studio.
- **Traction:** Not yet on the App Store (verified — no listing). Brand search "TeraMuse": US 450,000/mo, DE 165,000/mo — **treat as inflated**, almost certainly a keyword collision in Google Ads data; no way a pre-iOS indie tool has that brand demand.
- **User sentiment:** Runs aggressive SEO/comparison content ("Brain.fm alternatives," "best focus music apps 2026") — marketing-led, community presence not yet visible.
- **Source:** teramuse.com, omixapp.app (third-party review).

### 3. Pzizz
- **What it is:** Long-running generative audio app — algorithmically composed "dreamscapes" for sleep, naps, and a Focus module. The original Endel-style generative player.
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android.
- **Pricing:** Freemium + subscription.
- **Traction:** App Store 4.78, 9,651 ratings (US). Brand search: US 260/mo, DE 20/mo.
- **User sentiment:** Loyal niche; praised for actually working for sleep; seen as dated next to Endel.
- **Source:** itunes.apple.com id 915664862 (Pzizz, Inc.).

### 4. (Not Boring) Vibes
- **What it is:** Personalized soundscapes to focus / relax / sleep, matched to mood and weather. From Andy Allen's Not Boring Software — the strongest design-led indie in this space (award-winning, game-like interactions, haptics).
- **Platforms:** iOS.
- **Pricing:** Subscription (Not Boring suite bundle).
- **Traction:** App Store 4.85, 2,732 ratings (US).
- **User sentiment:** Beloved for craft and playfulness; complaint: subscription fatigue for the bundle.
- **Source:** itunes.apple.com id 1661440185.

### 5. FlowTunes
- **What it is:** Free indie Brain.fm-style focus music app (started as a web app; iOS app by solo dev Donald Crowe).
- **Platforms:** iOS, web.
- **Pricing:** Free.
- **Traction:** App Store 4.96 but only 57 ratings (US). Brand search US 390/mo.
- **User sentiment:** Reddit "free Brain.fm alternative" threads like it; too small to matter commercially yet.
- **Source:** itunes.apple.com id 6502615308.

### 6. Tide (潮汐)
- **What it is:** Chinese studio (Moreless) app combining Pomodoro focus timer + nature soundscapes + sleep. The "aesthetic calm focus" category leader in Asia.
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android.
- **Pricing:** Freemium + Tide Plus subscription.
- **Traction:** App Store 4.83, 8,307 ratings (US) — global base is much larger (China/Android). "Tide focus app" US search 30/mo.
- **User sentiment:** Praised for design and calm; Western awareness is low.
- **Source:** itunes.apple.com id 1077776989.

### 7. Spiritune
- **What it is:** "Music mood wellness" — functional playlists built on music-therapy/neuroscience principles, B2C + workplace/health plans angle.
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android.
- **Funding:** ~$2M raised (Pulse 2.0, Oct 2023: $1.7M; Fierce Healthcare, Feb 2025: "nearly $2 million") [verified].
- **Traction:** App Store 4.82, 231 ratings (US). Brand search US 170/mo.
- **User sentiment:** Users report real anxiety relief; tiny consumer base, B2B pivot underway.
- **Source:** itunes.apple.com id 1455800918, pulse2.com, fiercehealthcare.com.

### 8. sona (sona labs)
- **What it is:** "Music as medicine" digital therapeutic for anxiety — original music by Grammy-winning producers, neuroscience-backed, pursuing FDA-track clinical validation. Founder Neal Sarin.
- **Platforms:** iOS/Android historically; current iOS listing not found in US store (may be delisted — verify).
- **Funding:** Crowdfunded on StartEngine at a $20M valuation (KingsCrowd, 2022) [verified]; FDA clinical trial plans approved.
- **User sentiment:** Product Hunt reviews: calming, part of daily routine, helps sleep + work stress.
- **Source:** kingscrowd.com, dot.la, producthunt.com/products/sona-3.

## Ranked relevance to the venture (iOS generative techno focus music + interactive visual layer)

1. **Moongate** — proves iOS users pay ~$200k/mo for goal-based functional audio with zero brand demand; the paywall/onboarding playbook is copyable.
2. **TeraMuse** — closest concept rival: adaptive music reacting to user activity; watch its iOS launch.
3. **Pzizz** — the generative-audio veteran; proves the tech works, shows how it ages (dated UX, weak brand search).
4. **(Not Boring) Vibes** — the design bar for interactive/playful audio on iOS; exactly the "calm interactive layer" aesthetic.
5. **Tide** — proof the timer+soundscape+sleep bundle scales globally; a feature map worth stealing.
6. **FlowTunes** — free Brain.fm clone; shows the floor of the market is free.
7. **Spiritune / sona** — the clinical/B2B2C flank; relevant if the venture ever wants a health angle.
8. **Focus Friend / Forest** — prove gamified focus retention loops outpull audio quality; a music app could borrow the companion/room mechanic.
9. **Dark Noise / white-noise incumbents** — category gravity; big install bases, low differentiation, not the battleground.
10. **lofi.cafe / lofi.town / web lofi tier + dead lofi.co** — free substitutes and a warning: aesthetic focus web apps fail to monetize at ~26k MAU.

## Threat read (what this sweep means for the venture)

- **Copy:** Moongate's goal-first onboarding ("pick a goal → get a soundscape") and its evidence that aggressive iOS paywall funnels monetize functional audio without any brand. Also copy Not Boring's craft: playful haptics + reactive visuals are exactly the differentiator for the planned visual layer.
- **Avoid:** the lofi.co trap — beautiful free web focus products with ~26k MAU died; don't build a free-first web product. And don't compete on static ambient libraries — White Noise Lite / BetterSleep own that with 200k–390k ratings.
- **Opening:** nobody combines (a) genuinely generative *music* (not beats-over-loops, not static noise), (b) techno/electronic identity, and (c) a reactive interactive visual layer. Pzizz is generative but stale and sleep-first; TeraMuse is adaptive but not generative and not yet on iOS; Moongate monetizes but is binaural wallpaper. The generative-techno-plus-visuals slot on iOS is unoccupied.
