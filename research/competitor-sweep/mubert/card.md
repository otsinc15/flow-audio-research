# Competitor Card: Mubert

Researched 2026-09-01. Raw data in this folder (`itunes-*.json`, `dataforseo-*.json`, `sonar-pro-research.json`, `reviews-parsed.txt`).

## Identity

- Generative AI music company. Consumer app **Mubert: AI Music Streaming** (Mubert Play) plus B2B/creator products: **Mubert Render** (text/image-to-track generator), **Mubert Studio** (sample-contributor marketplace), **Mubert API** (licensing for apps/games).
- Platforms: iOS, Android, web (app + Render/API web tools).
- Company: **Mubert Inc.**, HQ Dover, Delaware, USA. Founded 2017 (Jan 17, 2017 per [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/mubert)). Team has Russian/Eastern-European roots; distributed.
- Pricing (consumer app): free with limits; premium ~$39.99/yr (per user reviews; upsell-heavy free tier).
- Pricing (Render, [verified on mubert.com/render/pricing](https://mubert.com/render/pricing), 2026-09-01): Ambassador free (25 tracks/mo, non-commercial); Creator $11.69/mo annual (non-commercial); Pro $32.49/mo annual (commercial); Business $149.29/mo annual (apps/agency). Tracks cannot go to Spotify/DSPs or Content ID.
- API pricing per [mubert.com/api/plans](https://mubert.com/api/plans): free trial tier; paid tiers up to ~$499/mo Startup and ~$36k/yr Enterprise (per F6S listing — treat as estimate).

## What users praise (App Store recent reviews)

- Generative variety and genre depth — the algorithm is tuned per genre, streams feel unique and endless.
  - 5★: "They really did different genres. The technology is built out properly for all of them. They didn't just put different sound libraries in the same algorithm they tweaked the algorithm for each genre."
  - 5★: "Mubert has very quickly become my go to music app when I want an endless evolving music stream. It's never boring but also not intrusive when I am working."
  - 5★: "I LOVE using this while I study. I used to run Lofi YouTube music but I found this app really does grow to your taste."
- Great for focus/work/writing immersion; no ads; lofi station repeatedly singled out.
- Learns taste via like/dislike training.

## What users complain about (App Store recent reviews)

- **Bugs and abandonment**: crashes, silent streams, broken favorites/sharing, features removed in updates. The app feels unmaintained.
  - 1★: "Open the app for the first time only to be forced to listen to a appearance in instrumental beat with a bone aching snare while you try to rush through and fill out the details… nothing loads properly you get bare minimum before the upsell."
  - 2★: "I was a paid customer but have left the service… too often I end up with audio I dislike that doesn't adapt to my feedback."
- **Cross-device/account premium failures**: paying on Android doesn't unlock premium on iOS; purchases not recognized.
- **No onboarding or docs**: "Unapproachable, obtuse… No tutorial or instructions." Confusing controls.
- **Missing basics**: no sleep timer (one user switched to Endel for this), no in-app volume, no landscape on iPad, no focus/pomodoro timer.
- Value doubt: 3★ "I can't really justify the $40/yr price tag for something I can get from youtube in about 20 seconds."

## Recent moves 2024–2026

- Consumer app is frozen: last iOS release Dec 2023. The "play music" side appears deprioritized.
- Pivot toward **B2B/creator tooling and Web3**: Render pricing/tiers reworked; a "Mubert Fuse" product for video soundtrack generation reported by third-party reviews [estimate — single source]; announced Polkadot / Web3 Foundation and Talisman-wallet partnerships around on-chain music data and creator rights (per Mubert blog / Coindesk press release via sonar-pro — [estimate, not independently verified]).
- No layoffs, acquisitions, or shutdown news found.

## Threat read

- **What to copy:** per-genre generative engines (not one algorithm with swapped sample packs) and the like/dislike taste-training loop — that's what loyal users name as the magic. Also the freemium "endless stream, no ads" hook.
- **What to avoid:** their failure mode is abandonment. A stale app, broken premium entitlements across devices, no onboarding, and missing table-stakes features (sleep timer, volume control, iPad landscape) burned a genuinely enthusiastic user base. Ship the basics and keep shipping.
- **Where the opening is:** Mubert vacated the consumer generative-focus space to chase B2B/Web3. Users literally ask for what the venture is building — focus timers, a visual layer ("wish you could draw a visualization and have the ai use that", "project this on a screen"), sleep timer. Nobody credible currently owns "Endel-type techno with high variety + interactive visual": Endel is ambient/wellness, Mubert's app is dead. The opening is a maintained, polished consumer app.
