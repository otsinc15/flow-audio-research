# Competitor Card: Endel

Researched 2026-09-01. Raw data saved alongside this file.

## Identity

- **What it is:** Generative "functional sound" app. Real-time AI soundscapes for Focus, Relax, Sleep, plus activity/movement modes. The category-defining brand Daniel's venture is compared against.
- **Company:** Endel Sound GmbH, Berlin. Founded 2018 (app released 2018-06-21).
- **Platforms:** iOS (main app, id 1346247457), macOS, Apple Watch, Android, web, Alexa. Also Apple Watch App of the Year (2020).
- **Second app:** "Endel Rituals" (iOS id 6749852909) — launched 2025-11-19. 2-minute mood-matched micro sound sessions. Free + IAP. 4.80★ but only 164 US ratings — very early.
- **Pricing:** Freemium, very limited free tier. Subscription tiers seen in App Store IAP: monthly ($2.99–$19.99 seen), annual $34.99–$119.99 (most complaints cite a $79.99 annual charge), lifetime ~$124.99. Pricing varies by storefront and promo. Aggressive lifetime upsell inside the app.
- **Version/activity:** v4.8.7, updated 2026-08-26 — actively maintained.

## What users praise (US store, 50 most recent reviews)

- Focus/study efficacy is the core win — doctoral students, ADHD users, long work sessions.
- Sleep soundscape works for many multi-year loyalists (6+ year users appear).
- Sound feels "designed," not like plain white noise or a looped track.

Quotes:
- 5★ (2026-08-21): "This app has been the best thing to happen to me when it comes to trying to focus when studying… with this app I have been able to keep my mind on the task at hand essentially until I'm finished."
- 5★ (2026-08-13): "All the benefits you need for initializing deep sleep and restoring brain wellness."

## What users complain about (dominant theme in recent reviews, by far)

- **Billing/cancellation dark patterns.** At least 8 of the 50 most recent reviews are about being charged ($79.99 annual) after canceling during trial, cancel flows that loop and obfuscate, charges outside the App Store, and unreachable support. Words used: "scam," "fraud," "preying on ppl w/ ADHD."
- **False advertising.** Social ads promote "8D" sounds that barely exist in-app ("only one 8D sound available"; "8D Odyssey… much slower and unexciting than what was advertised").
- **Low variety / repetitiveness.** Long-term users report the sound engine producing "unsettling, high-pitched noises" and having to stop using it.
- **Upsell pressure.** Lifetime-subscription popups even for paying subscribers.
- **Invasive autoplay** with headphones that can't be disabled.

Quotes:
- 1★ (2026-08-25): "The process of canceling is specifically designed to make it difficult. Incredibly scam-y."
- 1★ (2026-08-29): "I canceled… imagine my RAGE when I just now (days later) received notification that I was charged $79.99 for the annual subscription."
- 2★ (2026-08-24): "There really isn't much variety to choose from, and I also feel like the Focus, Sleep, and Relaxation options [are limited]."

Reddit (via sonar-pro, 2025–2026 threads): mixed-to-negative lately. Recurring threads titled "Endel App Scam," cancellation complaints, repetitiveness, cost. Common recommended alternatives: Brain.fm, myNoise, Noisli, Dark Noise, Portal, Focus@Will, Generative.fm, plain Spotify/YouTube.

## Threat read

- **Copy:** The proof that functional audio works — users genuinely credit Endel with focus and sleep outcomes, and the UMG partnership shows the "generative functional music" category has industry legitimacy. Real-time generative soundscapes (not playlists) is the right technical bet; multi-platform presence (Watch, widgets) drives daily habit.
- **Avoid:** Endel's monetization reputation is burning down its brand. Cancellation dark patterns, trial-to-annual surprise charges, and ad claims the product doesn't match are generating "scam" reviews in bulk in 2026 — and Apple-store billing trust is the whole ballgame for a subscription app. Also avoid the upsell-heavy UX.
- **Opening:** Variety and honesty. "Not much variety," "repetitive," and "ads don't match the app" are Endel's exact weak spots — a high-variety generative techno engine with transparent, App-Store-only billing and an ad promise the product actually keeps is a direct wedge. Endel's 8,100/mo US brand searches show real demand; its recent-review wall shows a large angry user base actively looking for alternatives.
