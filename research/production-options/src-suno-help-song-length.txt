 How long will my song be?
 Edited
 In two years, we've seen Suno's models generate just 30 seconds, to now generating up to 8 minutes before extending. 
 V2 is the oldest available model, and allowing for songs up to 1 minute and 20 seconds 
 V3 's maximum song length is 2 minutes 
 V3.5 and V4 improved further with 4 minutes in the initial generation.
 V4.5 and V5 can generate up to 8 minutes of music in one shot!
 To change between versions, locate the model dropdown to the right of the Custom mode option.
 You can also use Extend to add more music to the end of your song. Click Here to learn more about Extend!
