 (a) You can subscribe to or change a Music plan by visiting the ElevenLabs Pricing page and selecting “Music” or by contacting us.
 (b) If you upgrade to a higher-priced plan, all future use of the Service will be charged at the then-current price associated with that plan and all Output in your account at the time of upgrade will be subject to the upgraded plan. For example, if you upgrade from Scale to Business, all Output created under the Scale plan will subsequently be treated as if it were created under a Business plan. 
 (c) If you terminate your Music account or downgrade to a lower-price plan, Output will remain available in your account subject to the plan in effect when the Output was created. For example, if you terminate your Business plan account, you will continue to have access to and the right to use Output you created while a Business plan subscriber, but you will not be able to create new Output or modify existing Output using the Services. If you downgrade from the Business plan to the Free plan, you will continue to have access to and the right to use Output you created while a Business plan subscriber, but any new Output you create will be subject to the restrictions and limitations of the Free plan.
