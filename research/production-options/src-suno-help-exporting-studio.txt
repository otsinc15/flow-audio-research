 Exporting from Studio
 Edited
 Studio offers flexible export options to help you get your music out of Suno and into your production workflow. Whether you need the full mix, individual stems, or MIDI files for further manipulation, Studio has you covered.
 Using the Export Dropdown Menu
 The Export dropdown is your central hub for getting audio out of Studio. You'll find it in the top-right above the Timeline.
 Export Options
 When you click the Export dropdown, you can choose from several export scopes:
 Full Song Exports the complete mix of your project, including all tracks and processing.
 Selected Time Range Exports only the portion of the timeline you've selected. This is useful when you want to export a specific section or loop from your project.
 Multitrack Exports all tracks as stems within the context of your Studio mix, giving you maximum flexibility for mixing in your DAW of choice.
 Exporting Individual Clips
 Beyond the Export dropdown, you can also export individual clips directly from the timeline:
 Right-click (or long-press) on any audio clip in your project
 Select Download.WAV from the context menu
 The clip will download as a high-quality WAV file
 This is particularly useful when you want to grab a single element without exporting the entire project.
 Exporting MIDI from Stems
 One of Studio's most powerful features is the ability to extract MIDI from your audio stems. This opens up creative possibilities for remixing, reharmonizing, or simply learning from the generated melodies.
 How to Extract MIDI
 First, extract stems from your clip using the Stems panel
 Once you have individual stems (vocals, drums, bass, etc.), click on the stem you want
 In the context menu, select Get MIDI (costs 10 credits)
 Studio will analyze the audio and generate a MIDI file representing the melodic or rhythmic content
 This feature is especially valuable for:
 Recreating melodies with different instruments
 Analyzing harmonic progressions
 Building new arrangements from Suno generations
 Creating sample packs with both audio and MIDI components
 Export Quality
 All audio exports from Studio are delivered as high-quality WAV files, ensuring you have the best possible source material for further production work. MIDI files follow standard MIDI format specifications and can be imported into any DAW.
 Pro Tip : Before exporting, make sure to check your clip settings (tempo, transpose, speed) as these will affect the exported audio. You can find these settings in the Clip Settings panel on the right side of the interface.
