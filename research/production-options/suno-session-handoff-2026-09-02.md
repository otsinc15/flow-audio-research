## The sound we are after

- Hypnotic, minimal, dub-tinged techno. Think Plastikman "Consumed", Basic Channel, Deepchord, Donato Dozzy. Berlin, not Ibiza.
- 114 to 122 BPM, ideally **118 BPM**. Key **A minor** if Suno lets us choose.
- **The bass is the star**: deep, wide, fat, saturated, alive. A sub you feel plus a body you hear on earbuds.
- **Monotone on purpose.** A locked groove that repeats for minutes. Movement comes from slow filter and delay drift, never from events.
- **No drop, no build, no breakdown, no melody, no lead, no vocal, no risers.** Nothing that asks for attention.
- **Almost no pads.** No sustained washy chords. If there is a chord it is a short dry stab through a tempo-synced delay.
- **Dark up top.** Hats quiet and filtered, sitting behind the kick. Very little above 2 kHz.
- Some vinyl crackle, tape hiss or room noise is welcome.
- Always **instrumental**.

Things I have rejected many times before, so steer away from them: "paddy", "spacey", "too lively", "too much reverb", "sounds like a computer made it", "generic".

## Suno settings I want you to teach me

- Set **Instrumental** on. Lyrics field stays empty.
- Put the tempo and key into the style text, and use the exclude-styles field for the things above if the plan has it.
- Show me where **upload audio / cover / extend** lives on my plan, and how to base a generation on an uploaded clip.
- Show me how to download as **WAV**, and whether my plan has Suno's own **stem** export. If it does, we try it on one track so we can compare it with Demucs later.
- Tell me which plan I need for uploads, WAV and commercial use, before I generate anything. I will subscribe myself.
